I am a PhD Candidate at South China University of Technology, supervised by Prof. C. L. Philip Chen. I received my master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Pittsburgh in 2018, supervised by Prof. Zhi-Hong Mao. My work connects computer vision research with perception systems for intelligent vehicles and robots.

I have over eight years of experience in autonomous driving and AI engineering. Previously, I worked at Desay SV Automotive Singapore as a Principal Software Engineer / Discipline Manager, leading perception development across L2+ production programs and an L4 logistics vehicle platform. I later worked at Anker Technology Singapore on LLM/VLM-based multimodal diagnosis for robotic products.

My research interests include 3D Perception, BEV Representation, Multi-sensor Fusion, Depth Estimation, and Multimodal AI.

✉ Open to research collaboration and project-based computer vision work. Let's connect.

I collaborate with research and engineering teams on algorithm development, reproducible evaluation, and deployment-oriented prototypes.

🤝Research & Project Collaboration

Open to remote, project-based collaboration with research groups and engineering teams.

  • Computer vision & 3D perception: object detection, depth estimation, point-cloud processing, registration and sensor-fusion prototypes.
  • Multimodal AI: VLM application prototyping and evaluation using images, logs and other task-specific inputs.
  • Research engineering: baseline reproduction, method comparisons, evaluation pipelines and failure analysis.
  • Deployment: model integration and inference optimization for the agreed hardware and runtime.

We can start with a clearly scoped feasibility study or an algorithm module. Deliverables may include runnable code, environment instructions, experiment records and an evaluation report, agreed before the work begins.

To discuss a project, please share the problem, available data, expected deliverables and timeline.
